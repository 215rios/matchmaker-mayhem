How to Play

Create Person 1, pick what they want in a partner, choose Person 2's role, then select a partner and click Matchmake to reveal the wedding fate.

  • Choose one role and one style for Person 1.
  • Select up to 2 personality traits for Person 1.
  • Select up to 2 desired traits for Person 2.
  • Pick a role for Person 2 to filter the available partners.
